## vitalik-zk-snark (vitalik)

> How does a zk-SNARK differ from a zk-STARK?

### Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (Q4_K_M) — qwen3-4b-instruct-2507-q4km · chat-template · success · retrieval: none

zk-SNARKs and zk-STARKs are both zero-knowledge proof systems that allow one party to prove knowledge of a secret without revealing it. zk-SNARKs (Zero-Knowledge Succinct Non-Interactive Arguments of Knowledge) are more compact and efficient but require a trusted setup, while zk-STARKs (Zero-Knowledge Scalable Transparent Arguments of Knowledge) are transparent, do not require a trusted setup, and offer stronger security guarantees with better scalability.

## vitalik-quadratic-funding (vitalik)

> What is quadratic funding and what are its weaknesses?

### Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (Q4_K_M) — qwen3-4b-instruct-2507-q4km · chat-template · success · retrieval: none

Quadratic funding is a mechanism in decentralized funding where contributions to projects are scaled based on the number of contributors, encouraging diversity and reducing coordination costs. It allocates more funds to projects with broader participation, as the funding grows quadratically with the number of backers.

Weaknesses include potential inefficiency in funding highly popular projects that may not be well-designed, and the risk of "winner-takes-most" dynamics where a few projects dominate due to network effects. It can also underfund smaller, niche projects if they lack sufficient participation.
