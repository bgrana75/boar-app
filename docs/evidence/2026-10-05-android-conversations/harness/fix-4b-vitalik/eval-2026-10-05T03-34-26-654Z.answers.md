## vitalik-zk-snark (vitalik)

> How does a zk-SNARK differ from a zk-STARK?

### Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (Q4_K_M) — qwen3-4b-instruct-2507-q4km · chat-template · success · retrieval: none

zk-SNARKs and zk-STARKs are both zero-knowledge proof systems that allow one party to prove knowledge of a secret without revealing it. zk-SNARKs (Zero-Knowledge Succinct Non-Interactive Arguments of Knowledge) are more compact and efficient but require a trusted setup phase and are less transparent in their assumptions. zk-STARKs (Zero-Knowledge Scalable Transparent Arguments of Knowledge) are transparent, do not need a trusted setup, and offer stronger security guarantees with better scalability, though they are typically larger in size.

## vitalik-quadratic-funding (vitalik)

> What is quadratic funding and what are its weaknesses?

### Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (Q4_K_M) — qwen3-4b-instruct-2507-q4km · chat-template · success · retrieval: none

Quadratic funding is a mechanism used in decentralized governance and grant allocation where contributions to a project are weighted by the square of the number of contributors, aiming to encourage diversity and reduce the risk of over-reliance on a few large donors.

Weaknesses include inefficiency in allocating funds to small or niche projects, potential for high costs due to the quadratic formula, and difficulty in accurately measuring project value or impact, which can lead to suboptimal outcomes.
